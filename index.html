About

About me

I'm Ashok Sunuwar — known online as Jespyucha (my family name). This is where my work in markets, economics, and academia lives.

It started with a single question: how do markets actually work? The financial world is loud with jargon and prediction theater. This site is a deliberate move in the other direction — a place to document what I build, write, and study.

My commitment is radical transparency — documented process, honest reviews, and frameworks that survive uncertainty. Alongside my market writing, I publish my university essays and reports here as an open record of my work.
