About

I'm Ashok Sunuwar — known online as Jespyucha. I'm an undergraduate reading BA (Hons) Global Business Management at Coventry University London (graduating 2027), focused on strategy, consulting and financial markets.

My work sits at the intersection of business strategy and markets — from strategy-consulting cases (a BCG job simulation and a self-built consumer-credit case on Currys plc) to self-directed markets analysis, including a Nasdaq-100 analytics dashboard I built by teaching myself Python.

Alongside coursework and undergraduate research in organisational behaviour and international business strategy, I keep an open, evidence-first record of what I build, write and study. This portfolio is the structured professional view of that work.

Data · Markets · Self-initiated · December 2025 – Present

Nasdaq-100 Market Analytics Dashboard

Objective

Turn raw end-of-day market data into a decision-useful read on momentum and trend shifts across the Nasdaq-100.

Approach

Built an automated pipeline — fetch prices, compute price-change and relative-strength rankings across WTD/1W/MTD/1M/1Q/YTD/1Y, then attach the news driver behind each top mover — publishing a static dashboard after each market close.

Tools / Methods

Python · pandas · yfinance · HTML/JavaScript (Tailwind) · GitHub Actions · GitHub Pages

Outcome

A live, self-updating page that ranks gainers and losers and pairs top movers with their drivers. Live analytics tool — figures update daily; for educational purposes, not financial advice.

Informal Knowledge-Sharing in Remote Work — a Theoretical Review

Research question

Methodology

Theoretical review; a critical examination of visibility, accountability and workplace reciprocity as alternatives to distance-based explanations.

Developed the original concept of "visibility-contingent reciprocity" to explain declining informal knowledge-sharing. A research proposal / theoretical contribution — not an empirical finding.
